Personal channel tool

Zyntrivo Shorts Automation

A local tool that creates original fictional stories and publishes narrated short videos to the channel its owner authorizes.

Creates on the owner's PC

The tool writes fictional stories, produces synthetic narration, adds captions and combines them with licensed gameplay. Automated editorial and video checks withhold videos that fail its standards.

Uploads with permission

Google authorization lets the tool identify the owner's selected YouTube channel and upload completed videos to it. The owner controls publishing visibility and the upload schedule.

Personal use

This installation is for the ZyntrivoYT channel owner. This website is an information page, not a public service or a Google sign-in portal. It does not accept account passwords, OAuth credentials or video uploads.

Google account data

The tool requests permission to upload YouTube videos and read the selected channel's information. Authorization credentials and working files are stored on the owner's PC. See the privacy policy for the data used, its storage and how to revoke authorization.

All stories are fictional. Automated checks do not guarantee artistic quality, audience growth or YouTube monetization.
